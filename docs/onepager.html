A comedy game with a language model at the desk

THAT'S A FISH BARCODE

A gym scanner that accepts any barcode. A receptionist who remembers every fish you have ever scanned. She is not scripted — she is a live model with a memory ledger and a grudge that compounds.

The loop

Scan something absurd → the gate opens → she reacts. Then you come back tomorrow and she remembers. The meter is not a difficulty slider, it is a relationship: six stages, earned across 200 visits.

Why it travels

The game is the demo; the demo is the ad. Her best lines are improvised, so no clip can be pre-written — every streamer's run is unique footage.

The second product inside the first

FishBench — an LLM benchmark disguised as comedy. Score any model on how long it holds the bit:

  • Longest straight face
  • Fastest "Sir. Look."
  • Most creative threat
  • Fewest character breaks (assistant-voice leaks cost 50)

Every streamer becomes a QA tester. Every fish is a prompt injection.

Status

v0.1 shipped and playable. Zero dependencies, stdlib server, no build step. Runs keyless out of the box on an offline persona backend, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible model to make her live.
